Get started

Four steps.

Install

Run the installer. It installs for your user only and needs no admin rights. On a Mac, drag the app to Applications.

The app isn't code-signed. On Windows, SmartScreen may warn you: choose More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, choose Open Anyway in Privacy & Security. You can read and build the source yourself.

Coming from Codex Speak or Select to TTS? It's the same app under a new name. The installer replaces it and keeps your settings.

Sign in

Sign in to the Cursor desktop app, or install the Codex CLI and run codex login once. Without either, the app uses Kokoro if you download it, then the Edge and Windows voices, or the macOS voice on a Mac.

Take the tour

The tour opens on first launch. On a Mac, its first step asks for Accessibility access, which the app needs to read the selected text. Then try reading, rewriting, and dictation on sample text.

Tune it

Choose Settings… from the bar's More (…) menu, or click the tray or menu bar icon. Voices sets the source order and speed. Captions, Reading, Summarize, Writing, Dictation, and General hold the rest.

Good to know

Before you install.

The Codex voice is experimental

Codex's realtime API is marked experimental, and about 1 in 12 Codex reads in testing ended early or stayed silent. Silence moves the read to the next source on its own.

Apps that hide their selection

On Windows, the app reads only text that apps expose and never copies your selection, to protect your clipboard. Apps that hide their selection can't be read there. On a Mac, the app can borrow ⌘C for those apps and then puts your clipboard back. You can turn this off.

Keyboard selections on Mac only

Drag or double-click shows the bar. On a Mac, so do ⇧arrow and ⌘A. On Windows, keyboard selections don't.

Uninstall

On Windows, use Settings > Apps. This removes the app, its sign-in task, and the Kokoro model. Your preferences stay in %APPDATA%\harness-discussion.
